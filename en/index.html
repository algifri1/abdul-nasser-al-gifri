01 / Academic profile

A life in botany

From Abyan to international research institutions.

Abdul Nasser A.S. Al-Gifri was born in Abyan Governorate, Yemen, in 1957. He studied biology and chemistry at the University of Aden, and completed a Ph.D. in systematic botany and plant protection at the University of Silesia, Poland, in 1992.

In July 1995, he received an international diploma in herbarium techniques from the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, United Kingdom. His research interests include Yemeni flora, plant taxonomy, medicinal plants, and ethnobotany.

He has held academic and research posts at the University of Aden. The university's published profile also documents his work at the Zayed Complex for Herbal Research and Traditional Medicine, Abu Dhabi, from 1998 to 2005.
